Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you share on this site.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, email, optional phone number, number of guests and message.
  • Nominations: the nominee's name and portrait, plus your name and reason if you add them.
  • Dish suggestions: the dish and any details you add.
  • I'm Going cards: nothing. Your card is made on your own device, and your name is never sent to us.
  • Votes and backing: an anonymous cookie, so each browser counts once. It does not identify you.

How we use it

Only to run The Grand Riviera: replying to enquiries, building the ballot, planning the menu and counting votes. We do not sell your details.

Photos and nominees

The committee reviews every portrait before it appears anywhere. Location data is removed from photos when they are uploaded. Only nominate someone who has agreed, and if they are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree too.

To have a nomination or photo removed, use the contact form on the home page. The committee will take it down.

Tickets

Tickets are sold by Passly. When you buy, you are on their service and their terms and privacy policy apply.

How long we keep it

We delete enquiries, nominations and portraits after the event, once they are no longer needed.
