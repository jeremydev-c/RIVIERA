How old do I need to be?

Grand Riviera is for ages 16 and above.

What should I wear?

Black and metallic gold, cut long. The dress code applies to every guest and is upheld at the door, so dress for the room you are walking into.

What is included in my ticket?

Food, live music and the photobooths, plus your place at the crowning of Prom King and Queen.

How does the crowning work?

Anyone can put a name forward with a portrait. The committee reviews every entry and publishes the official ballot. On the night, every guest gets a ballot card at the door. Its one-time code gives one vote for Prom King and one for Prom Queen, and the crowns go to the highest count.
